# keeperstop-low-dive — checked 2026-09-27

Source: Christian Benjamin, [At Home Youth Diving for Goalkeepers](https://www.keeperstop.com/blogs/goalkeeper_drills-diving_extensions_reaction_saves/goalkeeper_drills-diving_extensions_reaction_saves-at_home_youth_diving_for_goalkeepers), Keeperstop.

Evidence: written session description, especially paragraphs beginning “We like to start” and “The second phase”. Video not visually reviewed. Youth session: stationary ball and controlled lowering from knees, then service; standing starts come later. Chapter 3 retains that sequence, not the higher/faster serves or game. Adult comfort, doses and standing alternative are the book's adaptations; kneeling is optional and not assumed suitable.
