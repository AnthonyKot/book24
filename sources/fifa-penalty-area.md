# fifa-penalty-area — FIFA Training Centre, "Goalkeeping fundamentals: defending the penalty area" (fetched 2026-09-27)

Session by a FIFA goalkeeping coach; full-size goal, several marked 5 m × 5 m areas, mannequins at varying distances, three keepers rotating. Four exercises from controlled technique to game situations.

1. Cut-back crosses from the byline, three deliveries (near post, mid-goal, back post); keeper dives low to block or gather.
2. Balls in behind, 1v1 blocking: coach plays beyond the defence; keeper rushes out to gather or confronts the runner; blocking shape.
3. Aerial balls, three scenarios: catch at the first post; deflect a cross to a teammate; far-post shot. Point: judge the flight, time the moment.
4. Low crosses and shots from the edge of the area into zones; keeper dives onto the ball with arms open.

Coaching points to carry into the book:
- Face the ball at all times and follow its movement.
- Short open steps, not jumps, to change position.
- Command the space by being assertive and closing distances.
- Blocking shape: upright upper body, arms slightly out.

Adaptation for a veterans' side (the book's own, to be confirmed): exercises 1 and 4 need one server who can cross; exercise 3 needs a server and, for the deflection scenario, one teammate; exercise 2 is chapter 4's pair form. Chapter 5 (crosses and corners) adapts 1, 3 and 4; chapter 3 (move, catch, recover) takes the short-open-steps point and the low dive.
