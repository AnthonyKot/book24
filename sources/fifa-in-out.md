# fifa-in-out — FIFA Training Centre, "Goalkeeper: in and out of possession" (fetched 2026-09-27)

Elite session, four goalkeepers, led by the FA's head of coaching; mini-goals, mannequins, rebounder, cones; a downloadable session-plan PDF exists (not fetched). Five parts.

1. 2v2 small-sided game, 18 m × 12 m with a halfway line and 5 m end zones; attackers score by dribbling over the end line or into mini-goals; defenders (keepers) win the ball with their hands. Point: distance awareness and body posture.
2. Diamond passing among the four keepers, five sequences from free play to being pressed; two keepers act as centre-backs. Point: distance and angles of support in possession.
3. 1v1 legs and footwork: rebounder 8 m out, coach tees up shots; keeper adjusts to the shooting angle (near-post lean vs far-post shuffle).
4. Closing down shooting angles: 25 m pitch, two goals; a keeper's goal kick goes through a support player to strikers, the opposite keeper closes the shot. The "3-metre rule" for deciding to advance.
5. Through balls and lofted passes on a half pitch with mannequins as defenders; coach plays the ball for a striker to chase; keeper holds a high position, then retreats. Point: decision-making from a high start.

Coaching points to carry into the book:
- Within about 3 m of the striker: smother, low posture, hands low. Beyond: tall, upright, ready for the shot.
- Near-post angle: lean with chest and front leg, back leg extended. Far-post angle: shuffle across and spread.
- Actions named: smother, block, spread, reaction save, advance to clear.

Adaptation for a veterans' side (the book's own, to be confirmed against the reader's answers): parts 3 and 4 need one server and a goal (or a wall for the solo form); part 5 needs two people; part 1 needs four and is the squad form of chapter 4. The 3 m rule and the two postures are the chapter 4 decision as stated by the source; whether veterans can cover 3 m at the same speed is the adaptation to test.
