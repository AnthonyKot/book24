# Between the Posts (working title)

A personal book on becoming a better goalkeeper in an amateur veterans' side. One reader. Read the chapters in the order they were written, which follows the reader's own matches:

1. [Review the match](chapters/01-review-the-match.md) — which situations should I learn from, and what information am I missing?
2. [Face the breakaway](chapters/04-face-the-breakaway.md) — when should I advance, hold my position or commit?
3. [Position before the shot](chapters/02-position-before-the-shot.md) — where should I stand as the ball moves, and when should I be ready?
4. [Move, catch, recover](chapters/03-move-catch-recover.md) — how do I keep control through a low save and the recovery?
5. [Deal with crosses and corners](chapters/05-deal-with-crosses-and-corners.md) — what can I claim, and what must the defenders cover?
6. [Organise the defence](chapters/06-organise-the-defence.md) — what should I say, to whom, and when?
7. [Start the next attack](chapters/07-start-the-next-attack.md) — when is a short pass useful, and when should I play longer?

The plan, the chapter register and the decision record are in [CONTEXT.md](CONTEXT.md). Source notes are in [sources/](sources/); match notes in [matches/](matches/). Diagrams and schemas are drawn by the book (SVG in `chapters/img/`), not photographs. Read on the site: https://anthonykot.github.io/book24/
