# BRIEF — the chapter contract

Length: 1,500–2,500 words. Markdown in `chapters/NN-<slug>.md`.

Order of beats:

1. **The situation** (one paragraph). A concrete moment from a match, told from the keeper's eyes, ending in the question the chapter answers. Where it is the author's own match, cite `matches/<date>.md`.
2. **What you can change and what you cannot.** Which parts of the situation are the keeper's, which are the defence's, which are luck. Short.
3. **The decision** (the chapter's one decision). The cue to look for, the choice it triggers, and what goes wrong on either side of it.
4. **The practice** (the chapter's one practice), in three forms: alone; with one teammate; with the squad. Each states the setup in things a veterans' side has (cones, one ball, a goal or a wall), the number of repetitions, and what "good" looks like. Credits the session it adapts.
5. **The observation** (the chapter's one thing to review after the next match). What to note, how to note it in one line in `matches/`, and what a run of such notes would show.
6. **Source line.** One line naming the sessions adapted.

Rules: exactly one decision, one practice, one observation per chapter. No drill lists. No chapter tells the reader to buy anything. Every distance is in metres and checkable on a pitch.
